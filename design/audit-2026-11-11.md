# Aurora Skin — delivery review, 2026-11-11

Scope: read-only review of the player/demo source, fresh 19-test production run, seven viewport captures, a narrow 16:9 embed, speed menu axe scan, Focus mode axe scan, touch gesture, offscreen behavior, error dismissal and one throttled local load. Product source was not changed during this review; audit scripts and evidence were added.

## Responsive geometry and layout

| Severity | Location | Before / observed | Required after | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HIGH | src/video/skin.css:73-84 | The 134px two-row dock overlaps the center play button at 320px (about 30px) and 600px viewport width (about 19px). In a 358x201px 16:9 embed the dock covers nearly all the center button and about two-thirds of the video height. | Layout must consider available height/aspect ratio as well as width. Hide the duplicate center control when it cannot fit, compact the dock and derive caption clearance from actual dock height. | Core viewing/interaction is obstructed in valid embed sizes; the square 390px demo does not prove the reusable skin is responsive. |
| MEDIUM | src/video/skin.css:11 | Captions have a fixed -160px controls offset. | Caption offset should follow dock geometry for short embeds. | Current value was visually checked in a square mobile demo, not every valid container height. The geometry is at risk in short 16:9 embeds; do not claim all such captions were visually validated. |

Evidence: audit-viewport-results.json; implementation/audit-320x740.png; implementation/audit-600x900.png; implementation/audit-embedded-16x9.png. No horizontal page overflow in any of seven tested viewports, but this does not imply absence of internal overlap.

## Verified again

- Production browser suite: 19/19 passed, no retries in this run.
- Axe: no selected WCAG A/AA-rule violations in the existing desktop/mobile suite, the additional open narrow menu scan or the Focus mode scan. This is not screen-reader certification.
- Touch double tap on the right region advances actual playback to about 10.6s.
- Scrolling the player offscreen sets atmospheric motion false.
- The official error dialog dismisses after OK.
- Navigating to credits/back restored a new page in this Chrome run; playback and ambient sampling worked. Browser back/forward cache restoration was not exercised (pageshow.persisted was false).
- No page errors in the seven-viewport sweep.

## Outstanding external verification

Physical Android/iPhone and Safari/Firefox; screen-reader behavior; profiling on actual low-powered hardware; Mux account/asset integration; public hosting origin and its range/CORS behavior; public source/project integration. No contest bonus, eligibility or judging score is established by this review.

## Assessment

The visual identity is coherent and more premium than the first pass, and the player is functional. It is not a final-ready entry while the narrow-layout obstruction remains. Prioritize responsive geometry, then poster loading, then error recovery and small-type refinement. Publish and validate Mux/real devices only after those corrections.

**Block**: HIGH findings remain in the reviewed responsive and constrained-load scope.
