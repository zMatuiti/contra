# Aurora Skin — design statement

Aurora Skin makes light part of the viewing experience while keeping the film central. Frosted controls, precise cyan/violet accents and a spatially sampled Living Aurora halo create a coherent cinema interface. The skin compacts for narrow players, measures caption clearance, and calms its ambient motion when reduced motion is requested.

Built with official Video.js 10.3.0 player state, controls, sliders, menus, hotkeys and gestures; the skin and optional ambient layer remain separate from the demo. The source is public under Apache 2.0, with separately licensed and credited sample media.

Demo: https://aurora-skin-player.vercel.app
Source: https://github.com/zMatuiti/contra

Validation: 23 automated scenarios passed locally and on the public origin in Chrome, including real playback and emulated touch. Physical-device, Safari and screen-reader testing remain outstanding.
